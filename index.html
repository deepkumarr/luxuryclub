PERFUMERY MASTERY

How We Differentiate Our Perfumes

On the Basis of Strength

Our perfumes are differentiated on strength—how far the aroma projects and how many particles others feel. We offer light to high strength options.

On the Basis of Lasting Effects

The durability of your perfume depends on the lasting impact. Our attars and Eau De Parfums hold distinct lasting benefits up to 24 hours.

On the Basis of Basic Elements

We produce our perfumes based on key formulas and natural essential oils like Ruby Red, Aqua Delight, White Leather, and Royal Oudh.
